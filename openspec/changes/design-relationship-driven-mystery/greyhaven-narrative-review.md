## Ruth: two motivational versions

**A — planned revenge and extraction.** Ruth learns of Adrian's culpability from the copies, attends to kill him and reclaim the original, and uses the farewell gathering as cover. This makes her choice deliberate before arrival and could explain a prepared early-collection lie. It also demands an unprovided plan for weapon, witnesses, concealment and departure. Keeping a visible shared-cupboard trail after careful preparation would feel artificially incompetent. Making her years of care tactical would flatten the character. A viable premeditated version would require substantial changes, so reject it for this focused revision.

**B — intended confrontation, chosen escalation (recommended).** Ruth knew Adrian for 32 years and believed the engineer's submission had been lost or excluded elsewhere, because he told her it never reached him. She suspected the board and the institution; she could still care for Mara/Owen and trust Adrian in ordinary life while being frustrated with his unsuccessful help. Iris's 14 October copy proves that his desk received the document, not yet why it disappeared. At20:18 he offers clerical-error wording and a later final discussion. She arrives to recover the original and obtain a signed acknowledgment, not to kill.

At20:37 his private admission of a conscious choice, followed by conditions on release and a threat to remove/destroy the original before tomorrow's deposit, destroys her remaining charitable explanation. She deliberately picks up the available weight and strikes. The escalation is unplanned; the violence is intentional and remains her responsibility. The proposed history explains anger without making violence necessary or justified. She could leave with the copies, seek intervention, refuse terms or expose the receipt.

She takes the original because preserving it was her objective before the blow; she cannot bear another erasure. She hides it temporarily under cloth on the familiar dry overflow shelf, separating it from her person while deciding what account to give. She then claims early authorized collection, rather than deny an object she may have been seen carrying. She knows Mara/Iris were on the terrace, but does not know Iris watched her threshold crossing or had read the seal earlier. Her blanket reception claim is a bad self-protective gamble after an unplanned killing, not a prepared watertight story or ignorance that witnesses existed. Do not give her a uniquely guilty gesture in the interview to explain this; the contradiction must be investigated. She rationalizes murder as stopping erasure and silence as sparing the household; allowing suspicion to fall on Owen exposes the self-interest in that rationalization. Her kindness remains real, but does not absolve her.

**Rejected third version:** accidental fall, medical episode or self-defense. It would undercut the retained intentional killing and turn responsibility into a convenient exonerating twist.

Public motive discovery is R-H (brother, grief, prior assurance, request for a signed account), G6 (original/pretrial receipt), and optional G7/I-P (recent copies, earlier clerical-error proposal, deadline). The final oral admission, exact threat and private rationalization are developer truth, not hidden material an ending may supply. No NPC confesses after a correct question.
