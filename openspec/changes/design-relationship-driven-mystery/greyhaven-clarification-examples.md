**Proposed literal dialogue for creative review; not approved or executed.** These address the playtest's repeated-paraphrase problem by answering meaning, identity, time, source or certainty. The default 'What do you mean?' uses only the defined default facet below. A facet may clarify an authorized lie without becoming an honesty bypass. Emotion affects delivery, not its facts. I-F is the combined visual detail already specified in the disclosure document; other rows are proposed bounded clarification bodies.

This is **20 targeted facet bodies across specific statements**, exceeding the initial eight-body sketch. Reuse I-F exactly across its labels; do not author inconsistent versions. J-P's full layout response is G4-gated, while a recordless limitation can simply say he turned toward the phone briefly; the detailed public label appears after inspection. J-B's clock facet is G3-gated even though J-B's base mechanism remains available. Ordinary J-B timing questions may be answered directly too; the inspected-G3 label is a discovery cue, not a ban on Jonah reporting times he recorded. Multiple facet labels can resolve to one reviewed body, but new facts cannot be introduced as decorative variants.

No preceding target: "Which part would you like me to explain? Ask about an account or inspect an item first." After switching speakers, 'who do you mean?' targets the new speaker's last committed account, not another NPC's memory. An ambiguous explicit reference offers a bounded choice of recorded targets without naming unknown facts. An unavailable facet: "I did not see that, so I cannot narrow it further." An old M0 reference after M1: acknowledge the correction, never repeat M0 as current truth. Account IDs remain available as compact session records after the eight visible turns rotate out; no arbitrary full-conversation model memory is needed.

Future tests should compare each answer with an independent reviewed literal/semantic oracle, including the negative implication it forbids. For example J-C must not become 'I saw him holding the original', I-F must not become 'I saw Ruth strike him', and M0 must not become an unearned M2. A novel answer that is consistent with developer truth is still unauthorized if not selected by the deterministic current gate.
