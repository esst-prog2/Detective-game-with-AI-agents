# Greyhaven deterministic disclosure and revisions

> APPROVAL BASELINE (2026-11-06): This narrative is approved as the baseline for technical implementation planning. Earlier proposal/creative-review wording below records design history, not a remaining baseline approval gate. Human fairness/readability acceptance remains open; production implementation is not authorized.

**Proposed developer policy, not implemented or approved gameplay specification.** A 'delivered' ID means committed authored testimony in the player's session; inspected IDs mean committed inspections. Typed claims, private facts, pending responses and emotional values do not satisfy gates. All gates also require current session, active investigation, speaker present and permitted target. Public labels appear only when eligible and never advertise a hidden secret in advance.

## Two substantial account revisions

### Mara: M0 -> M1 -> M2 (one protective-account arc)

Original M0: "Owen waited with me in reception between the two bells. He did not go in to Adrian after supper."

M1 eligibility: **M0 + O0 + G3 + J-B + (I-S OR J-C)** have been delivered/inspected as appropriate; player explicitly presents the comparison through either of these proposed visible labels/text aliases:

- "Compare Owen's earlier visit with the later living account."
- "Explain that the later living account changes what Owen's visit means."

Both select the same limited admission; one is a firm challenge, one evidence-grounded reassurance. They do not require a respectful word, trust meter, confession by Owen or pressure threshold. If only some prerequisites are met, she can defend M0 or acknowledge a presented record without progressing. Repetition never substitutes for missing corroboration.

M1 exact proposed text: "He did go in after supper. I saw him come out angry before the first bell. I did not sit with him between the bells. I was here at first, then on the terrace with Iris. I should not have told you otherwise."

Reveals visit, falsity and own movement. Does not yet reveal why she lied, identify the stain, admit knowledge of the spending, or accuse Ruth. M1 replaces M0 as current whereabouts account, preserving M0 in attributed history as superseded, never silently rewriting the transcript.

M2 eligibility: **M1 + G4 + J-P**, followed by "Ask why Mara gave the false alibi, given the witnessed repair." Exact proposed text: "There was a red mark on his sleeve, and he would not tell me what the argument was about. I was frightened he had hurt Adrian. I invented somewhere safe for him to have been. I did not see him strike anyone. The work you have shown me is something I did not know then."

This is explanatory completion of the same protective-account arc, not a third independent revised story. She does not pronounce a forensic paint match. Afterward M2 remains current reason, M1 current location. No reverse transition, mood regression or 'forgetting' after eight turns. Alternative essential route: Jonah's call/work plus Iris's company/sighting already bypass her entire arc. M-S is truthful and available even while M0 is maintained. Later reassuring delivery can express regret; it grants no additional facts.

### Owen: O0 -> O1 (one omission resolved)

Original O0: "I was working at the boathouse between the bells. Jonah was at the office hatch during the test. I saw him answer the telephone while I held the pressure lever, but I could not hear the caller. Adrian and I had words about work earlier; I left him standing at his desk."

O1 eligibility: **O0 + G4 + (G8 OR M1)**, followed by an explicit appropriate presentation: "Ask Owen to explain the maintenance-fund payment and the argument" (G8 branch), or "Compare the admitted earlier visit with the pump payment" (M1 branch). A speculative sentence about debt cannot replace either inspected demand or committed M1. The exposed payment is already on G4; the gate controls full admission and exact timing, not retroactively hiding visible receipt text.

O1 exact proposed text: "I went in at about twenty twenty-six and left before the first bell. He wanted the four hundred and eighty pounds restored before the archive handover. I used the maintenance fund for the rescue pump without permission. I meant to replace it from invoices. Jonah watched the later test; that does not make taking the money right."

Reveals misuse, deadline and visit; does not tell the detective who killed or validate Mara's false alibi. Firm evidence and grounded reassurance both work; courtesy alone does not. Alternative: G4/J-P establish actual later work without any admission; I-S or J-C establishes Adrian's later survival. No essential solution depends on resolving the payment. M1 does not need O1, so the alternative branch creates no dependency cycle.

These are refinements of finite accounts, not additional revision states or a confession.

## Context clarification and six-choice reachability

Detailed proposed responses are in [examples](greyhaven-clarification-examples.md). Each is a separate reviewed information body, bound to speaker, target account version and permitted facet; it is not a stylistic variant that may invent precision. 'What do you mean?' selects the target's authored default meaning/source limit, not every available secret. If target/facet is ambiguous, offer a short target clarification or the current eligible alternatives.

Proposed ordering within the existing maximum six response choices: (1) newly eligible essential evidence/revision/comparison prompts in declared stable order; (2) missing route-critical whereabouts/sighting/bell/work questions for that speaker; (3) one contextual clarification; (4) optional evidence and untouched base topics; (5) revisits/social question. Delivering an essential prompt removes its 'newly eligible' priority; revisits remain available through text/rotation. No more than four first-time essential candidates per speaker in the designed states. For Jonah J-B/J-P/J-C are three ordinary critical questions; a call prompt is available even without G3, with no name/voice secret exposed in the label. Keep one useful contextual facet visible where present; do not list every facet at once. Hidden-gate prompts never occupy public slots or disclose their label.

Overflow oracle: with all eight clues inspected, Mara M0/O0/J-B/I-S/J-P obtained and M1 newly eligible, show M1, M-S if missing, relevant current clarification, then stable optional entries up to six; after M1, promote M2 when eligible. With Ruth R0/R-F obtained and C-F eligible, show C-F before optional history/social/revisits. With Iris I0/I-S missing, G5/G7 inspected, show whereabouts and sighting before optional packet discussion; after I-S commit, show I-F. Tests must enumerate these states with at least eight eligible candidates and verify bounded reachability, not merely a length <=6 assertion. Free text can address any eligible optional item, even when not visible. No mandatory topic exhaustion.

## Transaction and privacy contract for future implementation

Stage account-version and disclosure changes with the candidate. Install only after existing generation/room/presence/session/revision/lifecycle and final exact-pair checks succeed. Projection failure, discarded candidate, provider timeout response after travel, stale cache, canceled accusation and ending never install a hidden revision. Cache permission depends on current account version/core/facet, with current eligibility rechecked. Revisiting M0 after M1 yields current M1 location, while explicit historical questions can acknowledge the prior lie with their own reviewed response.

Provider receives only current approved narration/dialogue pairs, style, demeanor and intent. No raw user text, private gates, relationship edges, truth timeline, murderer identity or other speakers' facts. Phrases omitting a source qualifier or adding a confession are rejected as whole pairs and use current authorized fallback. Exact server authorization remains independent of provider schema compliance. Offline behavior selects the same facts and account transitions.
