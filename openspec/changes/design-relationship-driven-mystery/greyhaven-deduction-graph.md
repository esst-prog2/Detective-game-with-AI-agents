# Greyhaven deduction graph and accusation standard

> APPROVAL BASELINE (2026-11-06): This narrative is approved as the baseline for technical implementation planning. Earlier proposal/creative-review wording below records design history, not a remaining baseline approval gate. Human fairness/readability acceptance remains open; production implementation is not authorized.

**Developer-only proposed reasoning oracle.** 'Sufficient' means substantial circumstantial support within the presented investigation, not mathematical uniqueness, legal conviction or a human fairness certification. A runtime murderer ID is never an evidential premise. Facts below distinguish material inspection from attributed accounts. Do not count two accounts derived from one document as two independent confirmations.

These are genuinely different key observation routes, not two orderings of an identical chain. They share physical evidence, operator/work testimony and Iris's limited company account; they are not disjoint investigations. No claim that the case remains fully supported if all witnesses and every common physical clue are refused. Other combinations need explicit reviewed reasoning before they can count as sufficient; do not implement an additive point score. A finite OR of reviewed support sets is the initial policy.

## Alternative suspects and confidence limits

Owen: argument, demand, red sleeve, omission and false maternal alibi make an initially sensible suspicion. Later living boundary plus witnessed repair undermine opportunity; explanation of paint/money is helpful but not required for the alibi. A fund misuse admission is not murder evidence.

Mara: false alibi and family financial stake justify scrutiny. I0 places her away from C with Iris through the window, followed by shared reception presence. She does not acquire Ruth's grievance by knowing her. This is testimony-based reduction, not impossible access.

Iris: unauthorized copying and hostility provide motive/access, but shared terrace interval and recorded earlier copying explain her secret. If the player suspects Iris colluded with Mara, neither route disproves that by logic alone; no evidence of collusion or beneficial shared plan is supplied, and the later folder contradiction instead converges on Ruth. Explicitly acknowledge this limitation.

Jonah: knows timings, may dislike Adrian, but Owen independently observes him across hatch through interval and accompanies discovery. J-P should include what Owen can corroborate in O-S if the player wants a symmetrical check. The minimum sets rely on Jonah's testimony plus G4, not a machine-certified alibi; an optional O-S increases confidence. Fabricating both log and call is possible in abstract, but lacks supporting evidence and does not explain independent folder/terrace accounts without further unsupported conspiracy.

Ruth: genuine grief and kindness neither convict nor acquit. Relevant access, false custody chronology and lack of corroborated interval account converge while alternatives reduce. Her assertion that she might have taken a document without killing is logically possible. The ending should say 'substantial circumstantial support', never 'only possible explanation' or 'the weapon proves it'.

## Ending policy and discovered-only explanations

Supported correct = complete A OR B, using committed obtained IDs; every explanation paragraph also checks its own prerequisites. Incomplete correct = Ruth selected without a complete reviewed chain; explain only obtained fragments and openly state missing corroboration, without supplying its content. Incorrect = another suspect, private wrong result, no murderer/motive/location leaks. Canceled = no outcome/mutation. Confirmation is final; no gate blocks a guess.

Examples: A without R-H/G7 must discuss custody and conflicting accounts, not Elias's prison death. B without I-S must never mention Iris seeing Ruth leave C. Without G1 never identify a possible chart-weight implement. Even with G1 the actual weapon is unknown to the player. Developer T08 and Ruth's private justification are never ending evidence. Source accounts superseded by M1/O1 remain historically recorded but cannot be counted as corroborating current truth.

## Adversarial route audit and independent sufficiency

[The narrative review](greyhaven-narrative-review.md) reconstructs the complete public sets separately and challenges each major inference. B is defended with J-C auditory presence plus M-S labeled placement/G1/G6, not with an unmentioned I-S/I-F sighting. A uses the visual account and Ruth's own custody claim; it cannot borrow the call's voices merely because G3 was inspected. Both use I0 only for the shared company check, and both use G3/G4/G6; independent key routes do not mean disjoint source sets.

A player's innocent-theft theory for Ruth remains logically possible. It must also account for her denied visit during a live-to-discovery interval in which the other interviewed people have corroborated whereabouts. That conjunction gives defensible circumstantial support; the case does not prove the exact blow or eliminate a conspiracy involving several fabricated accounts. If a reader finds an equally supported ordinary alternative after these checks, revise the route before approving a supported-ending rule. Neither proposed support set is a formal proof of guilt.
