# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Vary authored speech while preserving complete testimony
The system SHALL supply reviewed whole-utterance alternatives for authorized testimony, preserving every required assertion, denial, qualification, certainty limit and intentional lie. Default delivery SHALL omit the three reported personality announcements and SHALL NOT replace them with another routinely prepended signature. Original evidence and plain topic testimony SHALL remain unchanged. Case-record changes SHALL be limited to Alice's reviewed cufflink-recognition knowledge/testimony, her corrected concealment annotation and the corrected intended deduction summary. Meaningful authorized answers SHALL NOT become generic evasions to create variety. Alice's approved motive testimony SHALL remain verbatim; its annotation SHALL accurately describe the still-withheld letter and library activity, with no promotion of debt or private details to global knowledge.

#### Scenario: Answer unrelated questions without personality announcements
- **WHEN** a player asks several ordinary authorized topics of Alice, Ben or Clara
- **THEN** speech uses varied reviewed structures without repeating the reported catchphrases and retains the complete authorized content

#### Scenario: Preserve original compatibility and disclosure limits
- **WHEN** original plain testimony is required by a first ordinary-topic compatibility case or Alice answers her motive topic
- **THEN** the approved original wording remains available, Alice's motive remains verbatim, and debt-letter/library/private-route details are not newly disclosed

#### Scenario: Preserve recognition limits in every delivery
- **WHEN** Alice's cufflink-recognition answer is delivered offline, under pressure, from cache or through the optional performer
- **THEN** all deliveries retain the identification, lack of a witnessed loss and inability to date the clue's arrival, without adding ownership certainty or guilt beyond the reviewed account
