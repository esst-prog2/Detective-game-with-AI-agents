# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Make authorized NPC speech and narration less repetitive through reviewed structural variation and observable quality evaluation while preserving the deterministic mystery and exact performance safety boundary.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Vary authored speech while preserving complete testimony
The system SHALL supply reviewed whole-utterance alternatives for authorized testimony, preserving every required assertion, denial, qualification, certainty limit and intentional lie. Default delivery SHALL omit the three reported personality announcements and SHALL NOT replace them with another routinely prepended signature. Original case records, plain testimony, solution and evidence SHALL remain unchanged. Meaningful authorized answers SHALL NOT become generic evasions to create variety. Alice's debt annotation conflict SHALL remain unresolved, with her approved motive testimony retained verbatim and no promotion of debt or private details to global knowledge.

#### Scenario: Answer unrelated questions without personality announcements
- **WHEN** a player asks several ordinary authorized topics of Alice, Ben or Clara
- **THEN** speech uses varied reviewed structures without repeating the reported catchphrases and retains the complete authorized content

#### Scenario: Preserve original compatibility and disclosure limits
- **WHEN** original plain testimony is required by a first ordinary-topic compatibility case or Alice answers her motive topic
- **THEN** the approved original wording remains available, Alice's motive remains verbatim, and debt-letter/library/private-route details are not newly disclosed

### Requirement: Keep exact current-pair authorization
Both narration and speech SHALL match one complete engine-authorized pair for the current speaker, testimony, intent, emotional eligibility and recent delivery context. Novel generated text, omitted required information, individually allowed fields in an unauthorized combination and stale pairs SHALL be rejected with deterministic fallback. The model SHALL NOT establish facts, memory, options or consequential actions. All existing output bounds, private-information exclusions and server-only credential/model protections SHALL remain intact.

#### Scenario: Reject an unapproved paraphrase or gesture
- **WHEN** model output changes even an apparently harmless word, adds a gesture, omits a qualification or replays an ineligible prior pair
- **THEN** no unapproved content is displayed, remembered or cached, and a useful exact approved fallback is used only if the investigation and session guards still permit the turn

### Requirement: Prefer recent-delivery variety in every performance mode
Offline, configured-provider and cached delivery SHALL apply the same current eligibility and repetition policy. Actual committed selections SHALL guide bounded per-NPC variety; rejected, canceled or stale candidates SHALL NOT consume delivery history. Equivalent eligible alternatives SHALL prevent identical consecutive speech when available, and recently used speech/opening structures SHALL be deprioritized without inventing text. Session and speaker histories SHALL remain isolated and private. Cache reuse SHALL NOT override current variety or emotional eligibility.

#### Scenario: Repeat a question across offline and cached modes
- **WHEN** a player repeats an authorized question and multiple equivalent eligible alternatives exist
- **THEN** recent delivery influences the current offered alternatives, the useful account remains intact, and an otherwise safe cached recent pair cannot bypass those permissions

#### Scenario: Discard delayed work without consuming variety
- **WHEN** travel, inspection, ending, session replacement or revision/reservation changes invalidate asynchronous work
- **THEN** its selected speech, narration, recent-delivery metadata, counters and cache changes are all discarded under the existing atomic commit guards

### Requirement: Express personality and demeanor through eligible wording
Alice's careful, nervous and dutiful voice, Ben's blunt pride and pressured explanation, and Clara's polished composure and sharper defensiveness SHALL emerge through reviewed sentence choices and relevant reactions. For a given authorized core, neutral and irritated delivery SHALL be observably distinguishable where both are supported. Emotional transitions and permissions SHALL remain unchanged; demeanor SHALL NOT unlock secrets, imply guilt, force confession or alter testimony. References to earlier statements SHALL require committed same-speaker context or the existing reviewed cross-speaker prerequisites.

#### Scenario: Compare neutral and pressured versions of one answer
- **WHEN** separate authorized conversation setups lead to neutral and irritated delivery of the same core
- **THEN** wording or structure differs in a personality-appropriate way while the complete factual account and its intentional deception or limits remain equivalent

#### Scenario: Ask about another account
- **WHEN** the existing Alice/Clara sighting prerequisites authorize the reviewed comparison
- **THEN** Clara can respond naturally to the challenge using her existing account without deciding which witness is truthful or creating a confession

### Requirement: Use sparse varied safe narration
The system SHALL support dialogue-only replies and optional harmless expressions, gestures, posture or pauses. Narration SHALL be contextually warranted and normally separated by at least two speech-only replies, with an explicitly authored first-established-comparison reaction permitted during cooldown; the model SHALL NOT bypass this limit. Null narration SHALL remain eligible. Recent gesture and family selection SHALL reduce repeated physical mannerisms. Narration SHALL NOT reveal inner secrets, imply guilt, describe detective actions or perform consequential events.

#### Scenario: Select narration repeatedly
- **WHEN** a configured selector always prefers non-null narration
- **THEN** cooldown turns offer only speech-only pairs, the sparse limit still holds, and actual selected gestures are tracked without recording unseen fallback gestures

### Requirement: Present demeanor without technical interruption
The browser SHALL show a short natural label using only the existing public demeanor value. The delivery-versus-truthfulness explanation SHALL remain associated with the label and accessible through unobtrusive keyboard/screen-reader help rather than an always-visible technical sentence. Numerical emotions, private policies and guilt indicators SHALL remain absent. Existing art, navigation, focus behavior and responsive dialogue presentation SHALL be preserved.

#### Scenario: Read the demeanor label and help
- **WHEN** a player reviews a conversation visually or with keyboard/assistive navigation
- **THEN** the visible label is concise, its truthfulness limitation is accessible, and no private emotional state or invented public state is displayed

### Requirement: Evaluate naturalness with reproducible evidence
The refinement SHALL include a fixed repeatable 18-question-per-NPC evaluation covering ordinary topics, repeats, clarification, authorized evidence and the existing comparison, with neutral and pressured examples. Offline and deterministic fake-provider modes SHALL be evaluated without paid calls. Reports SHALL contain actual inputs and outputs, repeated opening/gesture frequencies, structural variation, meaningfulness, complete testimony review and personality/emotion observations. Measured results SHALL remain distinct from proposed examples and subjective judgments.

#### Scenario: Apply quantitative and editorial acceptance
- **WHEN** the fixed evaluation runs after implementation
- **THEN** each 18-turn NPC/mode sequence has zero reported signature phrases, no normalized four-word opening above 4/18, at least three substantively reviewed structures, at least ten substantive openings, no avoidable identical consecutive speech, and at most six narrated turns normally separated by two speech-only replies, with any first-established-comparison exception recorded explicitly
- **AND** actual gesture IDs do not repeat among the last two narrated turns, no gesture family exceeds half the narrations when at least four occur, offline sequences include at least two warranted narrations, and every required testimony qualification and stance passes editorial review

#### Scenario: Preserve before-and-after evidence and disclose limitations
- **WHEN** a report compares the captured baseline with the refined system
- **THEN** both sides contain executed transcripts under matched scenarios, failed gates remain visible, source/mode provenance is recorded, and neither finite variants nor lexical metrics are presented as proof of unrestricted natural conversation or semantic truth
