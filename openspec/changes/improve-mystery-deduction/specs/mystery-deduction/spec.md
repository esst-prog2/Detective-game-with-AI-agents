# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Make the existing Ashcroft mystery explainable through obtainable evidence and attributed testimony, with conclusions that respect discovery and uncertainty.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Support a public-action deduction chain
The existing case SHALL provide an offline path connecting the contract, Alice's garden sighting, Clara's conflicting garden denial, and a witness identification of the inspected cufflink. Footprints SHALL support a possible route without identifying their maker. The accusation SHALL rest on cumulative circumstantial support, not motive, demeanor or a contradiction alone.

#### Scenario: Investigate without developer knowledge
- **WHEN** a fresh player follows available room, inspection and contextual questioning actions
- **THEN** the player can obtain every stated connection without private records, precise hidden wording, a model or exhausting all topics
- **AND** the case does not claim a uniquely proven timeline, footprint match or eyewitness murder

### Requirement: Identify the cufflink through bounded witness testimony
After inspecting the silver cufflink, the player SHALL be able to ask Alice to identify it through an engine-issued option or equivalent supported text. Her authorized response SHALL identify it as matching the pair Clara wore at dinner and SHALL state that she did not see Clara lose it and cannot date its arrival beneath the desk. Existing generic evidence answers SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Present the inspected cufflink to Alice
- **WHEN** the active player inspects the study cufflink and asks Alice about it in the study
- **THEN** the response supplies the reviewed recognition and both uncertainty limits, without private murder-route or debt-letter details
- **AND** recognition is a remembered design match, not certainty of ownership or a witnessed drop, and how the object reached the study remains unknown

#### Scenario: Attempt to bypass evidence prerequisites
- **WHEN** the player submits the recognition choice or typed request before inspection, from a remote room, or after ending
- **THEN** the existing guidance or structured rejection applies and no recognition testimony is committed

### Requirement: Make investigation follow-ups discoverable
The recognition option SHALL become available after cufflink inspection while Alice is present. The garden-account comparison SHALL require both committed sightings, regardless of collection order. Relevant choices SHALL remain reachable within the six-option limit and SHALL NOT advertise guilt. Player claims SHALL NOT satisfy either prerequisite.

#### Scenario: Collect sightings in either order
- **WHEN** both Alice's garden sighting and Clara's after-dinner garden denial have committed
- **THEN** the existing comparison is offered to Clara and returns her authorized account without declaring who is truthful

### Requirement: Explain a correct verdict from recorded support
A correct accusation SHALL retain its existing verdict and append a deterministic explanation based only on successfully inspected evidence and committed testimony. It SHALL attribute witness statements, distinguish observations from inference, and omit undiscovered links. A verdict without the core supporting chain SHALL acknowledge incomplete investigation rather than invent an established case.

#### Scenario: Close a thoroughly investigated case
- **WHEN** the player has inspected the contract and cufflink, received Alice's recognition and sighting, received Clara's garden denial, and confirms Clara
- **THEN** the ending explains motive, the conflicting accounts and the physical connection with their uncertainty limits
- **AND** footprints contribute only if inspected, and no confession or private escape route is revealed

#### Scenario: Guess or investigate only partially
- **WHEN** the player confirms Clara without all core support, including with no investigative actions
- **THEN** the verdict remains correct, only recorded support is explained, and the ending says the investigation did not establish the complete supporting chain

#### Scenario: Lose an old history entry
- **WHEN** supporting testimony has committed but aged out of recent dialogue before accusation
- **THEN** the bounded authored-ID record still permits the appropriate attributed summary, without presenting the recent window as complete history

### Requirement: Preserve finality and incorrect-accusation privacy
An incorrect accusation SHALL end play with the existing incorrect verdict, without naming the murderer or revealing uncollected clues or private exonerating alibis. Both endings SHALL remain stable detached results available through existing CLI, HTTP and browser text contracts. Confirmation SHALL warn that the choice ends the case without assigning a guilt score or requiring a clue checklist.

#### Scenario: Accuse an innocent suspect
- **WHEN** the player confirms Alice or Ben from an active investigation
- **THEN** the result supplies no Clara solution, private letter or unreceived testimony, and received notes remain readable

#### Scenario: Reconcile or attempt another action after ending
- **WHEN** the final result is read again or a delayed question completes
- **THEN** the original verdict and explanation remain unchanged and no stale testimony is added

### Requirement: Preserve authorized offline compatibility
New testimony SHALL pass the same speaker, evidence, exact-pair, cache and final-commit permissions as existing testimony. Model requests SHALL contain only current approved deliveries and permitted style. The complete repaired deduction path SHALL work offline across CLI and browser without promoting testimony to objective truth or sharing it with other NPCs.

#### Scenario: Attempt a forged ownership admission
- **WHEN** a provider supplies another speaker's recognition, an ownership claim in the wrong context, a confession or an unauthorized route
- **THEN** the whole output is rejected with the existing safe fallback while all session and stale-response protections remain enforced
