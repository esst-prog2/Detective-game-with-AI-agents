## Context

A-C provide immutable case identity, private policies/receipts and statement-specific facets. Full packages require six subjects and a solution; a preview needs a narrowly declared subject subset and an enforced no-verdict boundary.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Deliver D1-D10 offline in the actual browser/CLI/HTTP. Preserve full-case validation, Ashcroft's authored behavior and all transaction guards. Do not expose future Greyhaven facts, fabricate a killer or implement E/F.

## Decisions

Use greyhaven-preview, mode preview, and a closed previewProfile.subjects list binding each included speaker to explicit topics. Only preview v2 may be sparse; full mode rejects the profile. Preview data contains no murdererId or solution. Registry supplies mode/profile as trusted constructor context; clients cannot supply these fields. Engine rejects every preview accusation before grading or changing state.

Only M0 and O0 are base alibi topics. Their public kinship appears in dossiers. O1 requires committed O0, inspected G4 and G8 and explicit reviewed presentation. Mara has no revision edge or future core. Reviewed M0 source, O0 work and O1 explanation facets use exact approved speech; historical O0 clarification uses the already approved O1 response as its correction, without authoring new facts. Non-terminal preview instructions state the repair objective and limits, without a deduction score or success ending.

The browser binds preview mode from the fetched manifest and immutable session envelope. Existing question/choice/history/journal flows remain; accusation controls are suppressed and server protection is independent. Default Ashcroft selection and full-case client metadata checks remain.

## Risks / Trade-offs

Two witnesses cannot establish full murder opportunity/custody routes or unlock Mara's revision. A limited-topic excerpt needs clear scope rather than filler testimony. Narrative helpfulness and fairness require human playtesting beyond automated literal checks.
