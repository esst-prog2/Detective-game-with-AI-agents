## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Publish an isolated preview
The registry SHALL publish greyhaven-preview with only Mara, Owen, reception, boathouse and G4/G8. Ashcroft SHALL remain default. Preview metadata SHALL identify the excerpt without a solution, future testimony or developer-only timeline.

#### Scenario: Select the excerpt
- **WHEN** the player starts greyhaven-preview
- **THEN** a fresh case-bound investigation begins without other-case notes or state

### Requirement: Validate bounded preview packages
Only explicit trusted preview profiles SHALL permit declared partial subjects. Ownership, local references, exact performances, gates and case identity SHALL retain full validation. Full cases SHALL retain six base subjects and a valid solution. Preview packages SHALL contain no solution or murderer identifier.

#### Scenario: Reject unsafe authoring
- **WHEN** preview authoring adds a solution, undeclared topic, foreign reference or unsupported capability
- **THEN** construction fails before publication

### Requirement: Investigate the approved repair dispute
Mara SHALL retain approved M0 without a revision path. Owen SHALL begin with O0 and revise to approved O1 only after committed O0, inspected G4 and G8 and explicit presentation. Authored clarification SHALL preserve source limits, historical/current distinctions and existing atomic authorization. Pressure, raw claims and models SHALL NOT satisfy prerequisites.

#### Scenario: Earn the admission
- **WHEN** both records and O0 are obtained and payment is explicitly presented
- **THEN** O1 and its receipt/account revision commit together without solving the murder

### Requirement: Keep previews non-terminal
Every preview accusation SHALL return fixed accusation_unavailable without grading, lifecycle/revision changes or hidden facts. Browser and CLI SHALL clearly label the limited objective and unavailable verdict while allowing continued investigation. Ashcroft endings SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Attempt a final verdict
- **WHEN** a preview caller tries any accusation directly or through a client
- **THEN** no verdict or ending is created and the investigation remains active

### Requirement: Verify public play and narrative fidelity
Real-case offline engine, CLI, HTTP and desktop/mobile journeys SHALL cover legal and premature admission, facets, unavailable accusations, case isolation and rollback. A source audit and human-playtest checklist SHALL distinguish automated success from human acceptance.

#### Scenario: Return to Ashcroft
- **WHEN** the player starts Ashcroft after the preview
- **THEN** fresh original gameplay is installed with no preview testimony or evidence carried over

### Requirement: Keep case selection and observation review accessible
Active and ended investigations SHALL offer explicit new-case selection without changing an existing session's case. Canceling selection SHALL preserve the current investigation. Received observation review SHALL visibly reopen its actual details without inspection, evidence mutation or additional commands. Preview accusation unavailability SHALL be explained at its normal action location while server enforcement remains unchanged.

#### Scenario: Revisit the preview after a full case
- **WHEN** the player starts Ashcroft, starts a new preview, returns to Ashcroft and ends it, then selects the preview again
- **THEN** each start creates a distinct correctly identified fresh investigation and earlier sessions retain their identities

#### Scenario: Review received evidence
- **WHEN** a player activates observation review by mouse or keyboard
- **THEN** a visible focused reading dialog shows the received description and closing restores focus without issuing a command

### Requirement: Display complete public relationships
Character cards, overview and dossier readers SHALL display public relationship labels with their public target names. They SHALL NOT infer or reveal private relationships, display unresolved internal IDs or repeat an already described relationship. Public label formatting SHALL NOT change character knowledge or disclosure authorization.

#### Scenario: Read Mara and Owen dossiers
- **WHEN** a player reads their public character cards
- **THEN** Mara's relationships identify Adrian as brother and Owen as son, and Owen's identify Adrian as uncle and Mara as mother, without isolated pronoun labels
